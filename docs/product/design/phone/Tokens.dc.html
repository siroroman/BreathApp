Rules in use

  • The Phase is never carried by colour: the Phase name and the ring position say it. Lit and unlit arcs have the same width and differ in lightness.
  • Dots in front of the torus are fully opaque at their depth; back dots are fainter and are decoration. With Reduce Motion, opacity stays between 75% and 100%, so the shape stays above 3:1 in light mode.
  • Increase Contrast is not drawn here. Ship it as asset-catalog variants of fg2, outline and ring that are at least one step darker (light) or lighter (dark).
